# Honyomi 1.0.0 released

We are excited to announce the first stable release of **Honyomi**!

Honyomi is a local-first multi-format reader for HarmonyOS that lets you manage and read manga, novels, e-books, and videos in one place.

## Highlights

- **Local-first**: your library, progress, and settings live on your device – no account required;
- **Multi-format**: EPUB / MOBI / AZW / AZW3 / PRC e-books, local manga directories (folders, ZIP / CBZ), and video files;
- **Extensible**: add online sources by installing community extensions (`.honyomiplug`);
- **Progress sync**: optionally connect to AniList, MyAnimeList, and 6 other tracking services;
- **Privacy**: app lock, screenshot protection, and hardware-backed credential storage. The app itself collects no personal data.

# Reader settings

Settings for the **Honyomi** manga reader, novel reader, and video player. Changes made while reading are remembered per series. Global defaults live in <navEn to="reader" /> and <navEn to="player" />.

## Manga reader

- **Direction**: left to right, right to left, vertical pages, webtoon, continuous scroll, smart grip;
- **Animations**: curl, slide, and cover. Single-page horizontal mode also has 3D cube, stack, fade, 3D flip, and sphere. Webtoon and vertical pages do not use the page curl;
- **Dual page**: automatic in landscape, always on, or always off. The cover can occupy a page by itself. Webtoon has no auto page-turn;
- **Picture**: crop (separate for paged and webtoon), color filters, brightness, pinch zoom, preload, page numbers, background color, tap zones, volume-key turning, keep awake;
- **Skip**: chapters already read, chapters hidden by a filter, duplicate pages, blank pages;
- **Webtoon**: margin, page gap, and a lock against zooming out;
- **E-ink**: a flash on each page turn, with color, duration, and interval;
- A transition screen sits between chapters. A gap in chapter numbers is called out.

The reader can also run OCR on the current chapter and draw the translation on the page. Engines are configured under **Translation** in Settings.

## Novel reader and read-aloud

- **Mode**: scroll, single page left to right, single page right to left, or dual page. Animations are curl, slide, or cover;
- **Text**: typeface (default, serif, sans, mono), size, line height, margins, paragraph spacing, first-line indent, and merging blank lines;
- **Display**: brightness, light or dark, keep awake, volume-key turning;
- **In the book**: search and bookmarks stay on the device;
- **Read-aloud**: the system voice, with speed and voice selection. The sleep timer counts minutes or chapters. While it plays in the background, other screens show a mini bar.

Narration stays on the device. The text is not sent to a speech server.

## Video player

- **Gestures**: double-tap left or right to jump 10 seconds; swipe up or down on the left for brightness and on the right for volume; swipe sideways to seek; press and hold to boost speed;
- **Controls**: speed, picture scaling, episode list, lock, auto-next, skip opening and ending;
- **Subtitles**: toggle and font size;
- **Danmaku**: on by default. Size, opacity, area, speed, and color can be changed. A comment can scroll, sit at the top, or sit at the bottom;
- Picture-in-picture and casting are supported. After the BitTorrent component is installed, a torrent file can play while it downloads.

Danmaku matching uses dandanplay. Turning danmaku off stops sending the title for matching.

# Reader settings

Settings for the **Honyomi** manga reader, novel reader, and video player.

## Manga reader

Open a manga from your library to enter the reader:

- **Page-turn mode**: left/right page turns (tap / swipe) or continuous vertical scrolling;
- **Display options**: double-page mode, white-border trimming, brightness filters, reading direction;
- **Bottom toolbar**: chapter list, page navigation, settings panel;
- Reading progress and position are remembered automatically.

Global defaults can be adjusted in the <navEn to="reader" /> settings.

## Novel reader and narration

- **Typography**: font size, line spacing, font family, margins, theme, and background color;
- **Narration**: on-device text-to-speech with adjustable speech rate, continuous background playback, and notification controls;
- Chapter navigation and progress work just like in the manga reader.

Narration happens entirely on the device – no internet connection needed.

## Video player

Open a video chapter to enter the player:

- **Gestures**: double-tap the left/right area to rewind / fast-forward, swipe up/down for volume / brightness;
- **Controls**: playback speed, quality, episode list, lock;
- **Danmaku**: enabled by default, can be toggled at any time.

Global defaults can be adjusted in the <navEn to="player" /> settings:

- Danmaku on/off;
- Danmaku font size, opacity, display area, scroll speed;
- Playback speed and auto-play.

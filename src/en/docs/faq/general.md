# General

Frequently asked questions about **Honyomi**.

## What is Honyomi?

Honyomi (package name `fun.shuey.honyomi`) is a local-first multi-format reader for HarmonyOS that lets you manage and read manga, novels, e-books, and videos in one place. Online sources can be added by installing community extensions (`.honyomiplug`).

## What devices and system versions are supported?

Phones, tablets, and PCs running HarmonyOS 6.1.0 or higher.

## Does Honyomi collect my personal information?

No. The app integrates no analytics, tracking, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs, has no account system, and sends no data to any developer server. See the [Privacy policy](/en/privacy/) for details.

## What content formats are supported?

:::: tabs
== E-books
EPUB / MOBI / AZW / AZW3 / PRC
== Manga
Local manga directories (folders), ZIP / CBZ archives
== Video
MP4 / MKV / WebM / MOV / M4V
== Online content
Online sources are added via community extensions (`.honyomiplug`); manga, novels, and videos are all supported.
::::

## Where can I get help?

- Browse the [Guides](/en/docs/guides/getting-started) and [FAQ](/en/docs/faq/general) on this site;
- Ask on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ShueyYuen/honyomi/issues).

## What content can I find in Honyomi?

Honyomi does not ship or distribute any content. Online content is provided by third-party extensions you install yourself; the app itself is only a reader.

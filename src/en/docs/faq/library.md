# Library

Frequently asked questions about the Honyomi library and categories.

## How do I add a series to my library?

1. Go to <navEn to="main_browse" /> and select a source;
1. Browse using the **Latest** / **Popular** listings, or search for the series name;
1. Tap the series to open its details page;
1. Tap the **Add to library** button, and the series will appear in your <navEn to="main_library" />.

## How do I remove a series from my library?

Long-press the series in <navEn to="main_library" /> and choose **Remove from library**. You can optionally delete downloaded files at the same time.

## How do I organize my library?

- Use [Categories](/en/docs/guides/categories) to group series;
- Long-press a series to move it to another category;
- The library supports sorting and filtering by name, last read time, and more.

## What does the Updates tab show?

The <navEn to="main_updates" /> tab shows new chapters for series in your library. The app checks for updates periodically; you can also pull to refresh manually.

## What does the History tab show?

The <navEn to="main_history" /> tab lists your reading / watching history in reverse chronological order, making it easy to jump back into what you read or watched recently.

## Where do I enable the app lock and incognito mode?

- Incognito mode: read without recording history or updating progress;
- App lock: enable it in <navEn to="security-and-privacy" /> to lock the app with biometrics.

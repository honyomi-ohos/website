# Library

Frequently asked questions about the Honyomi library, updates, and history.

## How do I add a series to the library?

1. Open <navEn to="main_browse" /> and pick a source;
1. Browse **Latest** / **Popular**, or search for the title;
1. Open the series;
1. Tap **Add to library**.

Which category it lands in depends on **Default category** in <navEn to="library" />: automatic, one chosen category, or ask every time.

## How do I remove a series?

Long-press it in <navEn to="main_library" /> and choose **Remove from library**. You can delete its downloaded chapters at the same time.

## How do I organize the library?

- Group series with [categories](/en/docs/guides/categories). A long-press edits categories, and one series can belong to several;
- Sort in <navEn to="library" /> by title, chapter count, last read, last update check, unread count, series update date, chapter fetch date, date added, or random, ascending or descending;
- The view can be a compact grid, a comfortable grid, a cover grid, or a list. Column count can be fixed or automatic;
- Badges for unread, downloaded, local, and language can each be turned on;
- Swiping a cover can mark the series read. Either side can instead do nothing;
- **Update on Wi-Fi only** and **Update while charging only** skip library checks when the condition is not met.

The category tabs at the top show only the series in that category.

## What does the Updates tab show?

<navEn to="main_updates" /> lists new chapters from the library. The header can filter, open upcoming chapters, and refresh the whole library. Each series has its own check interval on its page.

The same screen has a **Calendar** segment for the Bangumi airing schedule, by weekday.

## What does the History tab show?

<navEn to="main_history" /> lists reading history newest first, so you can return to a recent series.

## What does incognito pause?

<navEn to="incognito-mode" /> is on <navEn to="main_more" />. While it is on, new history entries and reading-time stats are not written. Read markers and the page position on the series are still saved.

An extension’s own page can turn on incognito for that plugin only. It has the same effect, limited to that plugin’s sources.

## Where is the app lock?

Turn on **App lock** in <navEn to="security-and-privacy" />. Unlocking uses a lock-screen credential the system already has: PIN, face, or fingerprint. If the device has none of those, the switch stays disabled.

**Lock when leaving the app** can be immediate, after 1, 2, 5, or 10 minutes, or never.

# Smart updates

Frequently asked questions about update checks in Honyomi.

## How do update checks work?

The app visits the sources of the series in your library at scheduled intervals to check for new chapters, and shows the results in the <navEn to="main_updates" /> tab.

## Why is a series not showing new chapters?

Possible reasons include:

- The source site is temporarily unavailable or has changed its structure (the extension needs an update);
- The series has no new chapters on the site yet;
- Network connectivity issues.

Try pulling to refresh in the <navEn to="main_updates" /> tab.

## Are new chapters downloaded automatically?

No. Downloads must be started manually from the series details page or <navEn to="download-queue" />.

## How do I check for app updates?

The app does not update itself. Watch the [download page](/en/download/) or [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/ShueyYuen/honyomi/releases) for new versions.
